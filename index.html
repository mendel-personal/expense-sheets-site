Expense Sheets

A private household expense tracker built on Google Sheets.

Expense Sheets is a small web app that two members of one household use to record expenses in their own Google Sheets spreadsheet and to ask questions about their spending. It is not a public service, and only the household's two Google accounts can use it.

What it does

  • Add expenses: a phone-friendly form that adds a line item (item, amount, date, category, payment type) to the right monthly tab of the household's spreadsheet.
  • Ask questions: a chat where the household can ask questions about their expenses, answered by Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic.

How it uses Google data

The app runs on Google Apps Script with the permissions of the person using it. It reads and writes the household's own spreadsheet, uses the signed-in Google account's email address to check that the person is one of the two allowed users, and requests a short-lived identity token for the app's own Google Cloud service account so it can authenticate to Anthropic's API.
