Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Expense Sheets ("the app") is a private web app used only by two members of one household to manage their own expense spreadsheet. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and where it goes.

Data the app accesses

  • Your Google account email address, to check that you are one of the two people allowed to use the app and to label who asked each chat question.
  • The household's Google Sheets spreadsheet, which the app reads to show the form, monthly balances and recent entries, and writes to when you add an expense. The app also keeps the chat history in a hidden tab of the same spreadsheet. It only opens the spreadsheet named in the link you use, and only with your own Google permissions.
  • A Google Cloud identity token for the app's own service account, requested with your Google credentials, used only to sign in to Anthropic's API. It does not give the app access to any other Google Cloud resources.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the app's features to the household: adding expenses, showing balances and answering questions about the expenses. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

When you ask a question in the chat, the question, the recent chat history and the contents of the spreadsheet are sent to Anthropic's API so that its AI assistant, Claude, can answer. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and privacy policy. No data is sent to Anthropic when you only add expenses.

The app does not sell, rent or share data with anyone else.

Storage and retention

The app has no database of its own. Expenses and chat history live in the household's Google Sheets spreadsheet and can be viewed, edited or deleted there at any time. Short-lived access tokens (valid for minutes) are cached by Google Apps Script and expire automatically.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
